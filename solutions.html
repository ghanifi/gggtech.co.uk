Solutions vs Services

Managed programmes, not piecemeal fixes

Our individual services, such as guest WiFi, cabling, CCTV or PMS integration, solve a specific problem or deliver a specific project. Our solutions are different: they are managed programmes that bundle the right services, people and processes into one ongoing commitment for your hotel, with clear responsibilities and a single point of contact.

For most hotels, 24/7 IT Support is the foundation. You can then add Cyber Security or Disaster Recovery & Backup as their risks and compliance needs grow. IT Consultancy & Project Management runs alongside for openings, refurbishments and major upgrades.

Ongoing programmes are charged at a fixed monthly fee, quoted per property after a free assessment. Consultancy and one-off projects are scoped and quoted up front, so you always know the cost before work begins.

FAQ

Hotel IT solutions: your questions answered

What is the difference between a solution and a service?
A service is a specific piece of work or technology, such as guest WiFi, CCTV or PMS integration. A solution is a managed programme that combines several services with ongoing support, monitoring and reporting, so we take responsibility for an outcome such as uptime, security or recoverability rather than a single task.
Can I combine more than one programme?
Yes. You can combine 24/7 IT Support with Cyber Security and Disaster Recovery so that support, protection and recovery are handled by one team. We tailor the combination to your property after a free assessment.
How are the programmes priced?
Ongoing programmes are charged at a fixed monthly fee, quoted per property after a free assessment. The fee depends on the size of your hotel, the systems involved and the level of cover you choose. Consultancy and one-off projects are scoped and quoted before any work begins.
Which programme should a small hotel or B&B start with?
For most smaller properties, 24/7 IT Support is the right foundation. It gives you a UK-based helpdesk, 1-hour on-site response in London and proactive monitoring of the WiFi, network and systems you rely on. Security and backup can be added as your needs grow.
Do your solutions cover hotels outside London?
Yes. All four programmes are available to hotels across the UK. Remote support, monitoring and security services work the same everywhere, and we arrange on-site engineers wherever your property is.
Do you offer a custom programme?
Yes. Not every hotel fits a standard package. After your free assessment we can build a programme around your property, your existing suppliers and your in-house team, whether you need full outsourced IT or support for the team you already have.
