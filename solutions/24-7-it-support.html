The programme

A managed programme, not just a phone number

Most hotels already have someone to call when something breaks. A managed programme is different: we take ongoing responsibility for your whole technology estate, from front office and back office to network and guest-facing systems, and work to stop problems happening in the first place.

The programme bundles capabilities we also offer individually: our 24/7 hotel IT helpdesk, remote support, on-site engineers and IT support and maintenance. If you only need one of those, each service page explains it in depth. If you want a single accountable partner for all of them at a predictable cost, this programme is designed for you.

Moving to a managed programme is structured and low-risk. Cover starts with your most critical systems and nothing is switched off until it has been documented.

Problems it prevents

Hotel problems the programme is designed to prevent

Hotel IT failures are rarely exotic. They are the same handful of issues, usually at the worst possible moment. The programme targets each one.

Guest WiFi is the most visible of these. Our guide to hotel WiFi problems and how to fix them explains the common causes in detail.

Front desk workstation failures

A frozen reception PC during the evening rush. Monitoring flags failing disks and overdue updates early, and the helpdesk can connect remotely straight away.

PMS outages and interface errors

When the PMS stops talking to key cards, POS or the channel manager, we diagnose the fault and escalate to the vendor with the evidence they need.

Payment terminals dropping offline

Card terminals that lose connection usually come down to the network. We keep the payment network segmented, stable and aligned with PCI DSS.

Guest WiFi complaints

Dead spots, slow speeds and login portals that fail. We watch access points and bandwidth and fix issues before they reach your reviews.

Key card encoder faults

An encoder that will not cut keys means guests cannot get into rooms. We support the encoder, the PC it runs on and its PMS link.

Check-in peaks under pressure

Friday arrivals and conference check-ins stress every system. We schedule maintenance away from peaks and prioritise anything that slows the queue.

FAQ

Managed 24/7 IT support: frequently asked questions

How is this programme different from your 24/7 helpdesk service?

The 24/7 helpdesk is one component: a support line staffed around the clock. The managed programme bundles the helpdesk with proactive monitoring, patching, on-site engineers, hotel systems support and quarterly reviews, under one fixed monthly fee and one SLA.

How much does managed IT support for a hotel cost?

Pricing is a fixed monthly fee, quoted per property after a free assessment. It depends on the number of rooms, devices, systems and sites. There are no hidden call-out fees for work within scope.

Do you support our existing PMS and suppliers?

Yes. We work with the systems you already have and act as the single point of contact with your PMS, broadband, telephony and hardware vendors. We only recommend changes where they solve a real problem.

What happens if an issue cannot be fixed remotely?

An engineer attends site within the on-site time set out in your SLA. Attendance for in-scope issues is covered by the monthly fee.

Is support really available 24/7?

Yes. Our UK-based team provides support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and bank holidays, which are exactly the times hotels most often have no one technical on site.

How long does onboarding take?

It depends on the size and condition of your estate. Smaller properties can be onboarded quickly; larger or multi-site groups are phased so cover starts with the most critical systems first. Your proposal includes a timeline.
