The programme

Strategy and delivery under one accountable partner

Hotel technology projects rarely fail because of the technology. They fail because nobody owns the whole picture: the PMS vendor, broadband provider, cabling contractor, AV installer and interior designer each deliver their piece, and the gaps between them land on the general manager.

This programme gives you one accountable partner. Our hotel IT consultancy service provides the expert advice; the programme adds hands-on project management, supplier coordination and a long-term roadmap, retained so the person who planned your network is the one who signs it off. Typical projects include cabling and infrastructure, network and WiFi upgrades and PMS integration.

We have worked with more than 500 hotels since 2010, so we know the order in which things need to happen in a live property, and which jobs should never be scheduled for a Friday afternoon.

Engagement process

How a typical engagement runs

The same disciplined process applies whether you are replacing WiFi in one building or opening a new property.

  1. 1

    Discovery visit

    We spend time on-site understanding your property, your team and your technology challenges. Book a visit.

  2. 2

    Audit and findings

    A written report on what you have, what is at risk and what is holding operations back.

  3. 3

    Strategy and design

    A detailed plan including scope, timeline, budget and recommended solutions, with options where there is a real choice.

  4. 4

    Implementation

    We manage delivery and every supplier involved, scheduling work around occupancy and events to minimise disruption.

  5. 5

    Testing and go-live

    End-to-end testing of every system and interface before go-live, with cut-overs planned for quieter periods.

  6. 6

    Handover and roadmap

    Documentation, staff training and an updated roadmap, with the option to move into our managed 24/7 IT support programme.

Problems it prevents

Project problems the programme prevents

Most project issues surface on opening day or the morning after a cut-over, when guests are already at the desk.

Guest WiFi is where poor planning shows first. Our guide to hotel WiFi problems explains why surveys and capacity planning matter.

Front desk not ready on opening day

Workstations, printers, PMS logins and phones are installed and tested before the first guest arrives.

PMS migrations that lose data

Data mapping, trial migrations and interface tests reduce the risk of missing reservations or broken links to key cards and POS.

Payment terminals not ready

Terminals are ordered, connected and placed on a properly segmented network well before go-live.

Guest WiFi built without a survey

Access points designed for the building rather than guessed, avoiding dead spots and costly rework.

Key card systems that do not integrate

Lock system, encoders and PMS interface are specified together, so keys can be cut from the PMS on day one.

Go-lives during check-in peaks

Cut-overs are scheduled around occupancy and events, never into your busiest arrival window.

FAQ

Hotel IT consultancy and project management: frequently asked questions

How is this different from your IT consultancy service?

Our IT consultancy service covers expert advice and audits. This programme adds hands-on delivery: a named project manager, supplier coordination, testing and handover, plus an ongoing roadmap retained on a fixed monthly fee.

Can you manage a new hotel opening from scratch?

Yes. We can plan and coordinate the full technology fit-out, including cabling, network and WiFi, PMS and interfaces, telephony, payment terminals and front desk equipment, working alongside your contractors and designers.

Do you work alongside our existing IT provider and suppliers?

Yes. We can lead the project and coordinate your existing suppliers, or bring in specialists where needed. Our role is to make sure every piece fits together and is tested end to end.

How are projects priced?

Ongoing consultancy and roadmap work is a fixed monthly fee, quoted per property after a free assessment. Individual projects are scoped and quoted separately, with fixed-price options available once the scope is agreed.

Can you help us choose a new PMS?

Yes. We help you define requirements, shortlist and evaluate options, check integrations with your existing systems and plan the migration and cut-over.

Do we have to take ongoing support afterwards?

No. The handover documentation is yours to keep. If you would like continued cover, you can move straight into our managed 24/7 IT support programme.
