Hotel IT Support Areas: London & the UK

From our London base at 86-90 Paul Street (EC2A), we provide 24/7 IT support to hotels across every London district and throughout the UK, combining fast remote fixes with on-site engineers when a visit is needed.

One hotel IT team, wherever your property is

GGG Technologies has supported hotels since 2010 and today looks after more than 500 properties across the UK. Whether you run a boutique hotel in Mayfair, a business hotel in Canary Wharf or a country house in the South West, you get the same service: a UK-based helpdesk that answers 24/7, a 1-hour response to every issue, and engineers who understand hotel systems.

London is our home. Our office in Shoreditch sits within easy reach of the West End, the City, Westminster, Kensington and the Docklands, which is why so many London hotels rely on us for day-to-day support, WiFi upgrades, PMS issues and cyber security.

Outside the capital, we support hotels across the South East, South West, Midlands and East Anglia, as well as groups with properties spread across the country. Most hotel IT problems can be fixed remotely, so distance rarely slows us down, and when an issue needs hands-on work we arrange an engineer to attend your property.

The same standard everywhere

  • 24/7 UK-based helpdesk for staff, day and night
  • 1-hour response to every issue, in London and across the UK
  • Proactive monitoring of networks, WiFi, firewalls and servers
  • On-site engineers dispatched when a remote fix isn't enough
  • Fixed monthly fee, quoted per property after a free assessment

How Response Works

Remote first, on site when it matters

Most hotel IT issues, such as a locked PMS account, a WiFi access point that has dropped off or a misbehaving card terminal, can be diagnosed and fixed remotely in minutes. That's why every call starts with our remote team, wherever you are. When something physically needs attention, we send an engineer.

1

You call, email or we spot it

Your team contacts our 24/7 helpdesk, or our monitoring flags a problem first. Either way, we respond within 1 hour.

2

We fix it remotely

Our UK-based engineers connect securely to your systems and resolve the issue, keeping you updated until it's closed.

3

We dispatch an engineer

If hardware, cabling or a site visit is needed, we send an engineer to your hotel and keep the remote team on the case until they arrive.

In London

Our Shoreditch base means engineers can reach central London hotels quickly. Your agreed on-site response targets are set out in your support plan.

Across the UK

Outside London, remote support handles most issues. For on-site work we arrange an engineer to attend, and we plan scheduled visits and projects in advance.

FAQ

Coverage questions

Do you provide hotel IT support across all of London?
Yes. We support hotels in every London borough, from Mayfair, Westminster and Kensington to Shoreditch, the South Bank and Canary Wharf. Our office is at 86-90 Paul Street in Shoreditch (EC2A), close to central London's main hotel districts.
Do you support hotels outside London?
Yes. We support hotels throughout the UK, including the South East, South West, Midlands and East Anglia, as well as hotel groups with properties in several regions. Remote support and monitoring work the same everywhere, and we arrange on-site engineers when a visit is required.
How quickly will you respond if my hotel is outside London?
Our 1-hour response applies to every hotel we support, wherever it is. Our UK-based team starts working on the issue remotely straight away. If an engineer needs to attend, we arrange a visit and agree on-site response targets for your location as part of your support plan.
Can you support a hotel group with properties in different regions?
Yes. Multi-site groups get one helpdesk, consistent network and security standards across every property, and central monitoring, with on-site engineers arranged locally for each hotel.
Does it cost more to support a hotel outside London?
Every hotel pays a fixed monthly fee, quoted per property after a free assessment. The quote reflects the size of the property, the systems we support and the level of on-site cover you need, so you know the full cost before you commit.
My area isn't listed. Can you still help?
Very likely. The areas on this page are where we work most often, not a complete list. Call us on +44 20 3642 4958 or email info@gggtech.co.uk with your location and we'll confirm how we can support your hotel.

Don't see your area?

We provide 24/7 remote support to hotels anywhere in the UK and deploy engineers nationwide for on-site work, projects and installations. Tell us where you are and we'll confirm cover.
