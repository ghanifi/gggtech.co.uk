Hotel IT support in practice

Every property below runs around the clock, and every one has the same problem when technology fails: guests notice first. Our work for these hotels covers the systems that keep a property trading, from the front desk PMS and card terminals to guest WiFi, phones, CCTV and the network that ties it all together.

Many of our clients are in Croydon and South London, close to our London base, which is why we can have an engineer on site within one hour across Greater London. Properties further out are covered by remote support straight away and a 4-hour on-site target.
